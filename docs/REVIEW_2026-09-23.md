# Review of 2026-09-23: the code, the published bank, and what a Python port needs

*See also [ESTUDIO_MAURICIO_2026-09-23.md](ESTUDIO_MAURICIO_2026-09-23.md): the
same day's study of whether the code represents Mauricio (2006)'s model, with the
proofs (BUG-46 to BUG-48 come from it).*

*A deep review of `drvec` 0.10 ahead of its port to Python, and the first run of
the published bank (`benchmark/`) through the program. Nothing in `src/` was
changed: the defects found are registered in [BUGS.md](BUGS.md) as `BUG-23` to
`BUG-45` (and additions to `BUG-18`, `BUG-19`, `BUG-20`), each with a
reproduction in `tests/repro/repro.sh`. The comparison's scripts and full reports
are in `benchmark/drvec_runs_2026-09-23/`.*

---

## 1. How it was done

Seven reviews in parallel, each read-only on the repository and working in a
scratch copy:

| area | scope |
|---|---|
| A | `drvec.c` 1–2460: the model, the parameter vector, transformations, diagnostics, Hessian, bootstraps |
| B | `drvec.c` 2461–5373: the gate, the `.inp` writers, seeds, forecasting, Wald tests |
| C | `drvec.c` 5374–8801: the `.pre` route, IRF/FEVD, the report, the rank test, the CLI, `main` |
| D | the bridge to fue: `fue_bridge.c`, `fue_pre_reader.c`, against `fue.load()` and fue's C |
| E | the engine (`elfvarma`, `drvmlest`, `qnewtopt`, `nlatools`) and `diagnose.c`, against drvarma |
| F | the published bank: Johansen–Juselius (Denmark, Finland, UK PPP), UK consumption, Canada |
| G | the published bank: Lütkepohl e1/e3 and Rao's tables |

Every finding had to come with a run, a minimal reproduction or an unambiguous
code path; the rest was kept apart as *suspected* (§6). The reproductions of the
crashes, of the memory errors and of the central defects were then re-run
independently before anything was registered; `tests/repro/repro.sh` prints each
one.

**Battery.** At the start `make test` gave 255 passed, 1 failed — the register
check, because BUG-18 lacked the *How it was found* / *What it cost* fields. With
those filled in: **256 passed, 0 failed**. Note that the battery passes with every
defect below present: BUG-23 and BUG-28 in particular show two places where it
cannot see what it is meant to check.

## 2. What holds

- **The engine is the canonical one.** `elfvarma.c`, `drvmlest.c`, `qnewtopt.c`
  and `diagnose.c` are byte-identical to `drvarma_v.04.1`; `nlatools.c` differs in
  comments only. P3.1 stands. The BUG-13 and BUG-16 fixes are present.
- **The likelihood is right.** On 13 optima of the bank an independent exact
  Gaussian likelihood (a Kalman filter sharing no code with drvec) gives the same
  `logelf` to 1e-4, and at `r = 0` the independent maximum equals drvec's in all 9
  files checked. What goes wrong is optimisation and reporting, not the
  likelihood.
- **Box-Cox is fue's.** At λ = 1 drvec uses `refactor·y` (drvec.c ~5354), as fue
  does. The defect found the same day in drtran's C (λ = 1 applied as `y − 1`)
  is **not** here.
- **The forecast algebra is right**: `compute_psi_weights`, `forecast_core` and
  `level_error_map`, and the Wald degrees of freedom.

## 3. The published bank

drvec is exact ML; Johansen is conditional ML (reduced-rank regression). Levels
of logL are not comparable (`n − 1` against `n − K` observations) and rank
statistics are not expected to coincide. What is expected is the same rank on
clear cases, close β and α, and differences that can be explained. Mapping used:
drvec `p` = `ca.jo` `K`; case 2 = `ecdet = "const"`, case 3 = `ecdet = "none"`;
β normalised on the `Y1` block. drvec cannot express a restricted trend, seasonal
dummies or exogenous I(0) regressors; where the published specification uses them
the closest specification was run and labelled so.

| case | reference | drvec (q = 0) | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lütkepohl **e1** | r = 1; trace 75.00, 15.45, 6.08; β (−0.035, −0.928, 1) | r = 1; β (−0.028, −0.933, 1); α close | **reproduces** |
| Lütkepohl **e3** | r = 1 (trace borderline 34.30 vs 34.91) | r = 1; β differs on the rates, and an independent exact ML lands on drvec's β: a flat direction (0.31 logL) | reproduces, difference explained |
| **Finland** (JJ 1990) | r = 2 | r = 2; β close | reproduces, differences explained |
| **Denmark** (JJ 1990, M = 4) | JJ chose r = 1 (λmax 1, trace 0) | r = 0 (22.33 < 28.14); β, α close; optimum verified by every route | β/α reproduce; the rank is borderline at n = 55 |
| Denmark (M = 5, the benchmark file) | r = 2 | r = 0 from `-lrtest`, **r = 2 after repairing the fits** | BUG-25 |
| **UK consumption–income** | README says r = 1; `ca.jo` rejects r ≤ 1 (full rank) | 1+ (r = M not expressible); β −0.869 vs −0.868 | reproduces; the README row is wrong |
| **UK consumption** (Pokorny) | r = 2 | r = 2, but the two LRs have swapped rows since 2026-08-17 | BUG-25 |
| **Canada** (Pfaff) | r = 1, restricted trend | cannot express the trend; closest spec gives r = 0 where β is ill-identified | cannot express |
| **UK PPP/UIP** (JJ 1992) | r = 2 by trace, 0 by λmax, with seasonals + oil | on pre-adjusted data drvec agrees with Johansen on those data; pre-adjusting costs 8 points of λmax(0) | cannot express faithfully |
| **Rao 3** (lc, li, lw) | r = 1 | r = 1; β (−0.945, −0.054, 1) vs (−0.953, −0.050, 1) | **reproduces** |
| **Rao 1, 2, 5** | `ca.jo` r = 2, 3, 2 | r = 1, 2, 1 once the fits are repaired | differences explained by the initial-state term (rao2: +11.0 at r = 1, +4.0 at r = 3 ≈ 14 LR points); all borderline at 5 % |
| **Rao 4** | λmax r = 1 | r = 1 after repair; default `-lrtest` gave nonsense | BUG-25 |
| **Rao 6** | `ca.jo` r = 5 | no fit at any rank | BUG-43 |
| **Rao 7** | r = 2 | raw units: junk; rescaled: r = 2 | BUG-43 |

**The Rao "ranks" are not published results.** `tools/extract_benchmarks.R` runs
`ca.jo` on every column untransformed: `Raotbl1` carries `k` and its own
seasonally adjusted copy, `Raotbl3` three impulse dummies as I(1) variables,
`Raotbl4/5` are Perron's univariate trend-break data, and the result files quote
the pre-NA `n`. They are same-specification Johansen references, nothing more.
The Lütkepohl book and the JJ 1990/1992 and Pfaff sources are not in
`literature/`; their published values could not be checked against print.

**Reading.** Where the specification can be expressed and the fits reach their
optimum, drvec reproduces Johansen within what exact-versus-conditional ML
explains. What stands between it and a clean bank is not the estimator but the
search (`-lrtest` from one cold start, no fallback from a non-stationary start,
raw scales), and three things the program cannot yet express: restricted trends,
seasonal dummies, exogenous I(0) terms.

**Shared code.** `fue_pre_reader.c` is code-identical to drtran's, so BUG-39 and
BUG-40 are defects of the drtran binary too. BUG-41 and BUG-42 are in drvarma's
canonical engine and should be fixed there and propagated, as BUG-16 was.

**Documentation and tools found wrong** (not registered as defects of the program):
- `tools/compare_johansen.py` treats `coint_johansen(det_order = 0)` as the
  restricted constant; it is the unrestricted one (it reproduces `ca.jo ecdet =
  "none"` exactly). Only its rank column is affected.
- `tools/strip_comments.py` crashes on the latin-1 files (`elfvarma.c`,
  `drvmlest.c`); used in a shell pipeline it yields two empty files and a false
  "0 lines differ". The published P3.1 numbers are right: they came from `gcc
  -fpreprocessed`.
- The `Makefile` comment "drvec no llama a chisq() — usa GSL" is false: every fit
  reaches `chisq` through Hosking and JB.
- `ARCHITECTURE.md` §2: drtran's `drvmlest.c` divergence is a finite-difference
  covariance **and the `isfinite` guard of BUG-41**, not `opt_iters`/`report()`.
- `HOMOLOGATION.md` §2.1 marks the swapped UKconsumption pair ✔, and its
  "urca_denmark M = 5, r = 2, logL 828.8447, converges" is a termcode-3 point 33
  log-units below the optimum (BUG-25).
- `benchmark/README.md`: UKconinc is full rank by `ca.jo`, not r = 1; the Rao
  ranks are not published (§3); UK PPP fails in `ca.jo` because the oil series
  were passed as endogenous, not because of the dummies.
- `-m 2` at `q = 0` gives the same logL as `-m 1` although the header says
  "Conditional": drvec cannot produce Johansen's conditional likelihood itself.

## 5. What the port has to carry

### 5.1 The two row orders — the root of the whole BUG-17 family

Everything `vec_shootx` builds in VEC coordinates — `Lambda`, `F`, `Theta`,
`Sigma`, `Q` with `Q[1,1] = 1` — is in the **internal** order `[Y1; Y2]`, so the
variance normalised to 1 is `Y1`'s. The data, the names, `B2`, the mean, the IRF,
the forecasts and the residuals are in the **`.inp`/`Ybar`** order `[Y2; Y1]` =
`[nabla Y2; W]`. `inp2lam(i)` maps between them and **depends on `r`**. BUG-17,
18, 19, 23, 28 and 29 are all the same mistake in different blocks. The port
should have one permutation object, applied at every I/O boundary, and a test per
block that pins a label against a known DGP.

### 5.2 The parameter vector

Walked by hand in about seven places (`init_guess` twice, `vec_shootx` twice,
`gate_profile_seed`, the printer, `rolling_eval`); BUG-30 and BUG-32 come from
two of those walks disagreeing. Layout: mean (case 2: `E[W]`, r values; case 3:
`E[nabla Y2]` then `E[W]`, M values) | `Lambda` **row-major** (or `psi` under
`-alpha`) | `F_k` row-major (diagonal under `-diagar`) | `Theta_k` in the class's
pattern | `Q` diagonal 2..M then the lower triangle by rows | `B2` **column-major**.
`Lambda` and `B2` are laid out in opposite orders. Under `-warma` the same slots
mean W-lag coefficients. **One pack/unpack module in the port.**

### 5.3 Global state

Dimensions and data (`nser`, `nobs`, `nobs_raw`, `rawmat`, `datamat`,
`Y2_levels`), every model flag as a global, and hidden cross-function state:
`granger_sv` (a side effect of `vec_shootx` read by the report), `B2_fixed`
(reallocated by every `init_guess` — the root of BUG-31(2) and BUG-32),
`alpha_A`, `cond_resid`, `seed_*`, `gate_*`, `prof_hold`. The modes mutate
`global_r` and the class flags and do not always restore them; the bootstraps
swap `outputv` to `/dev/null`. The port should pass an explicit specification
object, and the termcode should be a return value, not parsed from the `.out`.

### 5.4 Numerics to reproduce exactly (or to change on purpose)

- `LOG2PI` is truncated to `1.837877066` (4.1e-10 low): ~2.5e-8 on mink's logL,
  enough to break 10-decimal golden values if the port uses `log(2π)`.
- `sigma2` is concentrated; `Sigma[1,1] = 1`; AIC and BIC are divided by `n`.
- `xitol = ±1e-3` by `-m`; the refactor rule `10^floor(log10(1/mean|x|) + 0.5)`
  clamped to [1e-6, 1e6].
- Seeds: `ar_ols_seed` on the full-sample mean, MA seeds 0.1, `Sigma` seed divided
  by `Sig[1][1]` floored at 1e-24; the `-seedgate` multiplier order.
- Bootstrap: 64-bit LCG with the PCG constants, `(state >> 33) & 0x7FFFFFFF`,
  Box–Muller with a cached spare, seed 987654321; quantile `stat[ceil(a·nok)]`,
  `p = (ge + 1)/(nok + 1)`. **`unsigned long` is 32-bit on Windows**, so the C
  stream already differs between platforms.
- Multistart: seed 20260818, amplitude `0.05·(1 + (k − 1) % 20)`.
- IRF horizon 10 if `n < 40` else 20; long-run horizon 400 with a 1e-10 stop;
  the `-warma` shrink ladder {1, .8, .5, .3, .1, 0}.
- `fdhess`: forward differences, step `eps^(1/3)·max(|x|, 1)`, objective
  normalised to 1, `cov = 2 f H⁻¹ / n`, penalty 1e10.

**Decide before porting** whether the port reproduces or fixes BUG-41(2)
(termcode 3 value), BUG-42(3,4) (portmanteau df, JB) and BUG-40 (ν truncation).
The existing Python ports already disagree with the C and with each other on
BUG-41.

### 5.5 What already exists in Python

Base the port on **drvarma's Python package**, which is what drtran-python uses:

| C | Python | checked against the C |
|---|---|---|
| `elf` (AS 311) | `drvarma/_as311.py:elf` (pure, ~400–2000× slower) and `drvarma/_engine.py:elf_c` (cffi over the byte-identical C) | yes, to ≤ 1e-13 on logelf |
| `raxopt`, `lnsrch`, `cdgrad`, `bfgsfac`, `fdhess` | `drvarma/_qnewt.py` (1-indexed, faithful, reproduces BUG-41) | yes |
| `est`/`objcfunc` | `drvarma/estimate_py.py:estimate_w_py` (has the `isfinite` guard) | yes |
| `hosking_test`, JB | `drvarma/diagnostics.py` (inherits BUG-42(3,4)) | a test exists; it fails today because `drvarma_v.04.1/bin/drvarma` is stale (built before its own BUG-16 fix) |
| `chisq` | `scipy.stats.chi2.sf` | differs by ~1e-4 and wherever BUG-42(2) applies |
| the `.pre` reader | `fue.load()` | **changes results** where BUG-38/39/40 apply — see below |

`fue/qnewtopt.py` is a 0-based port that already fixes BUG-41: do not mix it with
drvarma's if bit-for-bit agreement is wanted.

**Still to be written**: drvec's cast (`vec_shootx`), a generic `est(cast)`, the
`-fdhess` SEs, the roots and `R(k)` blocks, IRF/FEVD in levels, `forecast_vec`,
the rank tests and the `.out` writer.

### 5.6 The reader: `fue.load()` instead of `fue_pre_reader.c`

The port should read `.pre` files with `fue.load()` (it is the one reader of the
format in Python, fixed on 2026-09-23 for BUG-0017..0022). Three places will
change results on purpose, and each needs a test that says so: a fixed μ ≠ 0 is
honoured (BUG-38); seasonal and fixed-frequency factors enter (BUG-38); the
deterministic component uses fue's ν with its 40-lag truncation (BUG-40), unless
fue changes. Refuse explicitly what drvec cannot model: non-standard deterministics
(fue.load accepts them), `ornsop > 0` in the univariate evaluation, `D > 0` on the
`.pre` route. Lock λ = 1 with negative data in a test.

### 5.7 Tests the port needs and the C does not have

- labels against a known DGP (Γ, Θ, Q, P, residual files) — the battery passes
  with BUG-23 present;
- the `-weakex`/`-alpha` check with a working field (`$NF`, BUG-28) and an M = 3
  `-lrtest -weakex` case;
- the rank test's size (BUG-24) and the order, not just the presence, of the
  registered LRs (BUG-25);
- golden values: `logelf` per fixture at fixed parameters, `granger_smin`,
  `canonical_b2`, the first bootstrap draws, `Psi_0..5` and `G_0..5` on
  mink-muskrat, the Wald statistics, the `gate_contract` lines.

## 6. Suspected, not established

- The gradient stop is ~10⁴ looser than intended when the objective at the
  optimum is far below 1 (normalised to 1 at the start; `umstop` divides by
  `|f| + 1`).
- NaN parameters pass `choldcp` and `chekma` (the comparisons are false for NaN),
  which is the path to BUG-41's hang.
- `eigenqr` ignores `gsl_eigen_nonsymm`'s status and no GSL error handler is set:
  a non-convergence would `abort()`.
- `ludcp` singularity is never checked in `prelim_b2`, `warma_inverse`,
  `canonical_b2`: collinear `Y2` would give inf/NaN silently.
- The rolling origin uses a full-sample `elf` pass (a look-ahead of order θ^o;
  negligible on mink-muskrat).
- The mean lag is printed when the increments change sign; the `.pre` route warns
  only when `d = D = 0`; `-interv` ignores the file's calendar.
- `drvarma_v.04.1/bin/drvarma` is stale and crashes on IPC3 (it predates its own
  BUG-16 fix); drtran's `qnewtopt.c` `report()` has a dangling `else`;
  drtran-python `chi_test(first = 1)` divides lag k by `n − k + 1` where the C
  uses `n − k`. Outside drvec, recorded here so they are not lost.
